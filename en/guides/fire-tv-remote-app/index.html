Use your phone as a Fire TV remote

Comandoo has initial support for Fire TV: you can use your phone as a remote over your home Wi-Fi, but this brand has not been tested on a real TV yet. Below are the steps and what to set on the TV if it does not respond.

  • Initial support
  • 3 min read
  • Updated Oct 12, 2026
  • App version 1.24.2

Initial support. Uses the unofficial protocol of Amazon's remote app. Not yet tested on a real device. If you try it, tell us how it went with the Report button in the app or at suporte@comandoo.online.

What you need

  • An Android phone.
  • The TV (or streaming device) connected to Wi-Fi.
  • The phone and the TV on the same home Wi-Fi.

Works with: Amazon Fire TV Stick and Fire TV.

Step by step

  1. Install Comandoo on your phone

    Comandoo is a free Android app with no ads. It is coming soon to Google Play; the link will appear on this site as soon as it is available. No account needed.

  2. Turn the Fire TV on and keep the phone on the same Wi-Fi.

  3. Open the app and pick your TV

    When it opens, Comandoo looks for devices on your network and lists them. Tap your TV. If it is not there, tap Search devices or use Connect by IP.

  4. The first time the TV shows a 4-digit PIN. Type it in Comandoo.

  5. Use the remote

    Done: D-pad and OK, back, home, volume, mute and power are on the Remote screen. Buttons the TV does not accept are greyed out.

Common problems

First, tap the green connection bar in the app and choose Test connection: in a few seconds it checks the Wi-Fi, the TV and the pairing, and tells you what to do.

What is the code or PIN the device shows?

It is how the device confirms it is you pairing. It appears on the device screen and changes on every attempt, so look at the device right then and type exactly what you see. Android TV uses 6 letters or numbers, Fire TV, Panasonic and Philips use 4 numbers, and Kodi uses its web control password, and Sony uses a key (PSK) that you set yourself in the TV, under IP Control.

My device does not show up in the search. What now?

Check that the device is on (not just standby) and that your phone is on the same Wi-Fi, not a guest network or mobile data. Some brands need phone control turned on in the device settings (see your brand under Compatible devices, in Settings). If it still fails, use Connect by IP. If nothing works, tap Report and tell me the device brand and model.

The connection drops or takes long to come back.

Comandoo reconnects by itself when Wi-Fi returns and every 30 seconds while the app is open. If the device was fully powered off, it only answers again after it is turned on. If your phone saves battery too aggressively, Android may pause the app: in Android settings, set Comandoo to no battery restriction. If it keeps happening, tap Report.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need the original remote?

Not to pair: the code, or the lack of one, shows on screen and you only use the phone. The TV must be on and on Wi-Fi.

Does it work with my model?

Amazon Fire TV Stick and Fire TV. Support is initial and not yet tested on a real TV; if something does not work, use Report in the app.

Is it really free?

Yes. The remote has no ads and no sign-up. In the future, some extra features may become part of an optional plan, but the basic remote stays free forever.

Does it work over mobile data or away from home?

No. Your phone has to be on the same Wi-Fi as the TV, because commands go straight from one device to the other.
