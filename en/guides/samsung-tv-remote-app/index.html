Use your phone as a Samsung TV remote

Comandoo has initial support for Samsung TV: you can use your phone as a remote over your home Wi-Fi, but this brand has not been tested on a real TV yet. Below are the steps and what to set on the TV if it does not respond.

  • Initial support
  • 3 min read
  • Updated Oct 12, 2026
  • App version 1.24.7

Initial support. Community-documented protocol; not yet tested on a real TV. If you try it, tell us how it went with the Report button in the app or at suporte@comandoo.online.

What you need

  • An Android phone (Android 8.0 or later).
  • The TV (or streaming device) connected to Wi-Fi.
  • The phone and the TV on the same home Wi-Fi.

Works with: Samsung Smart TVs with Tizen (2016 models and later).

Step by step

  1. Install Comandoo on your phone

    Comandoo is a free app for Android 8.0 or later, with no ads. It is coming soon to Google Play; the link will appear on this site as soon as it is available. No account needed.

  2. Turn the TV on and keep the phone on the same Wi-Fi.

  3. Open the app and pick your TV

    When it opens, Comandoo looks for devices on your network and lists them. Tap your TV. If it is not there, tap Find devices or use Connect by IP.

  4. The first time the TV shows a prompt asking to allow Comandoo. Accept it with the TV remote.

  5. If the prompt does not appear, check the TV's external device manager settings and turn access notifications on.

  6. If the TV asks for permission every time the app connects, open the TV's Device Connection Manager, then Access Notification Settings, and choose First Time Only.

  7. The phone and the TV must be on the same network (same subnet). Samsung blocks control coming from another network, such as a guest Wi-Fi.

  8. Use the remote

    Done: D-pad and OK, back, home, volume, mute and power are on the Remote screen. Buttons the TV does not accept are greyed out.

Common problems

First, tap the green connection bar in the app and choose Test connection: in a few seconds it checks the Wi-Fi, the TV and the pairing, and tells you what to do.

My Samsung TV asks for permission every time I connect. How do I stop that?

On the TV, open Settings > General > Device Connection Manager > Access Notification Settings and choose First Time Only. If this phone is already in the Device List, delete the old entry and authorize once more. After that the TV remembers the authorization and the app does not need to ask again.

The device refuses commands even though it is on. What could it be?

Samsung and Panasonic only accept commands from devices on the same network. If the phone is on a guest Wi-Fi, another router segment or mobile data, the TV refuses. Connect the phone to the same Wi-Fi as the TV and try again.

My device does not show up in the search. What now?

Check that the device is on (not just standby) and that your phone is on the same Wi-Fi, not a guest network or mobile data. Some brands need phone control turned on in the device settings (see your brand under Compatible devices, in Settings). If it still fails, use Connect by IP. If nothing works, tap Report and tell me the device brand and model.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need the original remote?

Only for the first pairing: the request on the TV has to be accepted there. After that, the app reconnects on its own.

Does it work with my model?

Samsung Smart TVs with Tizen (2016 models and later). Support is initial and not yet tested on a real TV; if something does not work, use Report in the app.

Is it really free?

Yes. The remote has no ads and no sign-up. In the future, some extra features may become part of an optional plan, but the basic remote stays free forever.

Does it work over mobile data or away from home?

No. Your phone has to be on the same Wi-Fi as the TV, because commands go straight from one device to the other.
