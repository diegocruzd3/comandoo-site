How to type passwords and searches on your TV from your phone

Open the field you want to type in on the device, like YouTube search or an app password. In Comandoo, open the Type tab, write with the phone keyboard and tap Send to the device. If you prefer, dictate with the microphone button.

  • 3 min read
  • Updated Oct 12, 2026
  • App version 1.24.7

What you need

  • Comandoo already connected to the device (see the first connection guide).
  • The phone and the device on the same Wi-Fi.

Step by step

  1. Open the text field on the device

    Use the app’s D-pad to get to the search or password field and select it with OK, until the TV keyboard shows up.

  2. Open the Type tab

    In Comandoo, tap the Type tab. The phone keyboard opens.

  3. Write and send

    Write the text on the phone and tap Send to the device. The text goes to the field open on the device.

    On LG, the button is called Search YouTube on the device.

  4. Or dictate by voice

    Tap the microphone button and say the text. You can also ask directly by voice, for example “search cats on YouTube”.

Voice commands that save typing

Hold the voice button and say, for example: open Netflix, search cats on YouTube, volume 15, channel 5, HDMI 2 or type followed by the text. Numbers can be spoken in words. Say help to see the full list.

Common problems

First, tap the green connection bar in the app and choose Test connection: in a few seconds it checks the Wi-Fi, the TV and the pairing, and tells you what to do.

The text does not show up on the device

Make sure the text field is open and selected on the device before sending. Some apps use their own keyboard that does not accept outside text; in those, use the D-pad to pick the letters. If it happens in every app, tap Test connection on the app’s green bar.

Why are some buttons dimmed or do nothing?

Comandoo only shows what your device accepts through its protocol. If a button is dimmed, that function does not exist on that device or brand through the app. If you think it should work, tap Report and tell me the model.

The connection drops or takes long to come back.

Comandoo reconnects by itself when Wi-Fi returns and every 30 seconds while the app is open. If the device was fully powered off, it only answers again after it is turned on. If your phone saves battery too aggressively, Android may pause the app: in Android settings, set Comandoo to no battery restriction. If it keeps happening, tap Report.

Frequently asked questions

Does it work in every app?

It works with text fields that use the device’s system keyboard. Some apps have their own keyboard and do not accept outside text.

Can I paste a password copied on my phone?

Yes: paste it into the Type tab field like any text field on the phone, and tap Send to the device.

Is it really free?

Yes. The remote has no ads and no sign-up. In the future, some extra features may become part of an optional plan, but the basic remote stays free forever.
