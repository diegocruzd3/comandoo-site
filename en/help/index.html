Understand the Test connection result

Test connection (on the green connection bar, or in the More tab, under Help and support) checks in order: Wi-Fi, saved device, same network, device answer, pairing and speed. The title shows the first problem found. Find the title you saw on your phone below.

“No Wi-Fi”#

The phone is not on Wi-Fi. The remote needs the same network as the device, and mobile data will not do.

What to do: Tap Open Wi-Fi settings and connect the phone to the same network as the device.

Open the guided check

“Local network access blocked”#

Android is blocking the app from talking to the device. In Android Settings, open Apps, this app, Permissions, and allow Nearby devices.

What to do: Follow that path in Android settings and run the test again. Only happens on Android 17 or later.

Open the guided check

“No saved device”#

There is no device connected on this phone yet.

What to do: Tap Find devices and pick yours from the list.

Open the guided check

“Phone and device seem to be on different networks”#

This happens with guest Wi-Fi, repeaters or when the device is on another band of the router. Put both on the same network.

What to do: Put the phone on the same Wi-Fi as the device (same network name and band, 2.4 or 5 GHz) and tap Find devices.

Open the guided check

“The device did not answer”#

No control port of the device opened. Check that the device is on (not on standby) and on the same Wi-Fi. If the router changed its address, Find devices finds it again.

What to do: Turn the device screen on and tap Find devices. Some brands need network control turned on in the device settings.

Open the guided check

“No saved pairing”#

The device has not authorized this phone yet. Tap the device in the list and accept the request that appears on its screen.

What to do: Tap Find devices, pick the device and accept the request or type the code shown on its screen.

Open the guided check

“Slow answer”#

Buttons may lag. Moving the router closer to the device or using the 5 GHz band usually helps.

What to do: If you can, move the router closer to the device or put both on the 5 GHz band.

Open the guided check

“All good: the phone and the device are talking.”#

Network, device, pairing and speed all passed.

What to do: If a button still does not work, run the guided check with the problem "Connects but doesn’t respond".

Under the result you always see Test again and Send with a report, which attaches the result to your report. Lines saying "Not tested because an earlier step failed" go away once the first problem is fixed. Lines about ports 6466 and 5555 are information for support only.

App FAQ

The same answers you see in the app, under Settings. Each question has its own link to share.

Get started

What is Comandoo and which devices does it work with?#

Comandoo is a remote control that uses your home Wi-Fi. Today it controls LG (webOS), Roku, Samsung (Tizen), Android TV and Google TV, Fire TV, Sony Bravia, Panasonic Viera, Philips, Kodi and TVs on the Vestel platform. Only LG has been tested on a real TV; the other brands are in early testing. The full, up-to-date list is in Settings, under Compatible devices. The app is independent and not affiliated with any manufacturer.

How do I connect for the first time?#

Turn the device on and keep your phone on the same Wi-Fi. Open Comandoo and tap the device in the list, or tap Find devices. The first time, the device shows a request or a code: accept it with the device's own remote or type the code in the app. After that the app reconnects by itself.

Connect

My device does not show up in the search. What now?#

Check that the device is on (not just standby) and that your phone is on the same Wi-Fi, not a guest network or mobile data. Some brands need phone control turned on in the device settings (see your brand under Compatible devices, in Settings). If it still fails, tap Use IP and type the device's IP. If nothing works, tap Report and tell me the device brand and model.

What is the code or PIN the device shows?#

It is how the device confirms it is you pairing. It appears on the device screen and changes on every attempt, so look at the device right then and type exactly what you see. Android TV uses 6 letters or numbers, Fire TV, Panasonic and Philips use 4 numbers, and Kodi uses its web control password, and Sony uses a key (PSK) that you set yourself in the TV, under IP Control.

How do I find my device's IP address?#

In the device network settings, under Wi-Fi or Network, look for Network information or Connection status: the IP shows as four numbers separated by dots, like 192.168.1.42. You can also see it in your router's list of connected devices. Tap Use IP and type it, without http:// or a port.

The connection drops or takes long to come back.#

Comandoo reconnects by itself when Wi-Fi returns and every 30 seconds while the app is open. If the device was fully powered off, it only answers again after it is turned on. If your phone saves battery too aggressively, Android may pause the app: in Android settings, set Comandoo to no battery restriction. If it keeps happening, tap Report.

Can I turn the device on from my phone?#

It depends on the device. On LG, turn on Power on with mobile device (or Power on via Wi-Fi) in the TV and use Turn the device on over the network, after the app has connected once. On other brands, turning on over the network depends on the model and many can only turn off. Turning off from the app works on most.

The device will not connect. How do I find out why?#

Tap the green connection bar (or the More tab › Help and support) and choose Test connection. In a few seconds Comandoo checks the Wi-Fi, the saved device, whether the phone and the device share a network, whether the device answers, the pairing and the speed, and tells you what to do. If it does not help, tap Send with a report to include the result.

My Samsung TV asks for permission every time I connect. How do I stop that?#

On the TV, open Settings > General > Device Connection Manager > Access Notification Settings and choose First Time Only. If this phone is already in the Device List, delete the old entry and authorize once more. After that the TV remembers the authorization and the app does not need to ask again.

My Roku connects but the buttons do nothing. What can I do?#

On newer Roku TVs (since 2024) mobile access comes set to Limited, and the TV refuses commands. On the TV, open Settings > System > Advanced system settings > Control by mobile apps and set Network access to Enabled. Then tap Try again in the app.

My LG connects, but the touchpad and buttons do not work. What can I do?#

Some recent LG updates (webOS 26) accept pairing without allowing control of the buttons. In Settings, tap Pair again and accept the request that appears on the TV. If the TV refuses the app, update the app and try again.

The device refuses commands even though it is on. What could it be?#

Samsung and Panasonic only accept commands from devices on the same network. If the phone is on a guest Wi-Fi, another router segment or mobile data, the TV refuses. Connect the phone to the same Wi-Fi as the TV and try again.

My Panasonic (or Toshiba, Telefunken) does not connect as a Panasonic. What can I do?#

Some models from these brands are made by Vestel and do not use Panasonic's remote control (port 55000). They accept commands on another port (56789), with no PIN. Tap Find devices, or tap Use IP and type the TV's IP: the app recognises it by itself. This compatibility is initial, not yet tested on a real TV.

My device brand is not on the list.#

Many TVs run a system we already cover: TCL, Philco, AOC, Multilaser and Semp are often Roku or Android TV, and Toshiba and Philips can also be Android TV. Try Find devices. If the TV does not show up, tap Report with the brand and model or a photo of the label so I can see if it can be added.

Use

Why are some buttons dimmed or do nothing?#

Comandoo only shows what your device accepts through its protocol. If a button is dimmed, that function does not exist on that device or brand through the app. If you think it should work, tap Report and tell me the model.

How do I type text on the device?#

Open the Type tab. On LG, with a text field open on the device (for example, YouTube search), what you type shows there live; with no field open, tap Search YouTube on the device to open YouTube with the search. On other brands, open the field on the device, type on the phone and tap Send to the device. You can also dictate with the microphone button.

Which voice commands can I use?#

Hold the voice button and speak, for example: turn the TV on, open Netflix, search cats on YouTube, volume 15, mute, channel 5, pause, back, HDMI 2 or type some text. Numbers can be spoken in words. Say help to see the full list.

How do I control the device without leaving another app?#

In the More tab, turn on Floating button. Android asks once for the permission to display over other apps. The button stays on top of any app and opens the remote when tapped. To hide it, close the button or turn it off in the same place.

How do I send a YouTube video to the device?#

In the YouTube app, open the video, tap Share and choose YouTube video on device (Comandoo). The connected device opens the video. On LG, Roku, Android TV and Samsung it opens right away; on other brands the app opens YouTube on the device and you pick the video there. If the device only opens YouTube, tell us in Report so we can adjust it.

What are Scenes and how do I create one?#

A scene joins several steps in one tap, such as turning the device on, opening an app, setting the volume or turning it off. In the More tab, under Scenes, tap a scene to run it, or New scene to create your own: give it a name and add the steps. Steps run in order and the scene stops at the first one that fails, saying why. To turn the device on, Comandoo must already know its MAC address (connect once while the device is on).

Can I control the device with my phone locked?#

Yes. In Settings, turn on Lock-screen remote. A pinned Comandoo notification then shows Vol −, Vol +, Mute, Pause and Power off, even while the phone is locked. The app only connects to the device when you tap a button and disconnects soon after, to use little battery. Power off asks for a second tap within 5 seconds, to avoid accidental taps in your pocket. It needs the notifications permission.

Why does the order of my apps change?#

With Smart favorites on (Settings, Control), Comandoo notes, only on your phone, how often you open each app and at what time of day. The apps you use most at that time come first, and recents follow your use. Old habits fade with time. To keep a fixed order, turn the option off: your favorites go back to the order you chose.

How does the arrive-home notice work?#

It is optional (Settings, Control, Notify me when I get home). When the phone joins a Wi-Fi network, Android tells the app, which checks whether the saved device answers on the network. If it does, a quiet notification appears: Control the device? Tap to open the remote. It uses no location or GPS, only the device's own answer. It shows at most once every 6 hours, never between 10 pm and 7 am, and not if you just used the app. It needs the notifications permission.

Can I have the remote on my home screen?#

Yes. Long-press an empty spot on your phone home screen, choose Widgets and add Comandoo: it has volume, mute and power off. There are also tiles in Android quick settings (pull down the top bar and edit the tiles).

Share

How do I send a photo or video from my phone to the device?#

In your phone gallery, open the photo or video, tap Share and choose Comandoo. The device opens the file over your home network. Phone and device must be on the same Wi-Fi and the app must be connected. This was tested on LG; on other brands it may not be available.

The app

How do I change the color and language?#

In Settings, tap Appearance to choose the theme (light, dark or system) and the accent color, and Language for Portuguese, English or Spanish.

What are notifications for and how do I turn them off?#

They tell you about answers to your reports and important announcements. In Settings, tap Notifications to turn each type on or off. If none arrive, check that Android allows Comandoo notifications and that battery limits are not restricting the app.

Privacy and reports

What are anonymous statistics?#

They are optional and start off. If you accept (in the question after your first connection, or in Settings, Privacy and data), the app sends only the device brand, which step worked or failed (search, pair, connect or command), the reason in fixed categories, and the app and Android versions. It does not send IP or MAC addresses, device or network names, texts, or an identifier of your phone. It helps us see which brand fails most and fix it first. You can turn it off any time, and anything not yet sent is deleted. The data is deleted from the server after 6 months.

What does the app send over the internet?#

Commands to the device, the device list, addresses and favorites stay only on your phone and your home network. The app downloads the compatible device list, this FAQ and announcements from the internet, and talks to the announcements and reports server, which receives a random install code and whether you opened or tapped each announcement. A report is sent only when you decide: it carries your text, the app version, phone and Android model, language, device brand, connection state and recent events (with the end of the IP, MAC addresses and e-mails hidden) and up to 2 shrunk photos, without location or date. There are no ads and no account. The full policy is in Settings, under Privacy policy.

How do I report a problem or ask for a new device?#

In the More tab › Help and support, tap Report a problem (or the Report button on help screens). Tell what happened, choose the brand and write the device model. If you cannot find the model, attach a photo of the device or of the label on its back and I will identify it. The answer arrives in the app and as a notification, and then I ask you to test again.
