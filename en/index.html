Your data stays with you.

Here’s the short version. The full text, covering everything the app uses, is in the privacy policy.

  • Commands stay on your networkThey go straight from phone to device over Wi-Fi, never through the internet.
  • No account, no adsThere’s no login, and the app doesn’t use the advertising ID.
  • Voice through Android itselfVoice search uses your phone’s speech recognition. The app only receives the text.
  • Reports only when you send themIf you send a report, it includes technical details, and the app hides IPs and e-mails first.
  • Statistics only if you wantThey start off. If you turn them on, nothing in them identifies you or your device.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to install anything on the TV?

No. Comandoo uses the network features the device already has. It just needs to be on and on the same Wi-Fi as your phone.

Does it work over mobile data or away from home?

No. Your phone has to be on the same Wi-Fi as the device, because commands go directly from one device to the other.

My device doesn’t show up. What should I do?

Check that the device is on and that both devices are on the same Wi-Fi (some routers split 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz into separate networks). If it still doesn’t appear, send a report from the app with your TV’s brand and model.

Is the app paid?

No. The remote is free and has no ads. In the future, some extra features may become part of an optional plan, but the basic remote stays free forever.

Does it work with any device?

It works with Wi-Fi-connected Smart TVs and streaming devices on the platforms listed above. Devices without a network connection, or that only accept infrared remotes, won’t work.

Who makes Comandoo?

Agência D3, from Belém, Brazil. Comandoo is an independent app with no ties to any TV manufacturer.
