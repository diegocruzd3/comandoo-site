Ready in under a minute.

No cables, no receiver, no app on the TV. Your phone talks to the TV over your own network.

  1. Same network

    Connect your phone to the same Wi-Fi as the TV.

  2. Pick your TV

    Open Comandoo. It finds the TVs in your home and lists them.

  3. Confirm on the TV

    Accept the request on screen. Some TVs ask for a code.

Your phone must be on the same network as the TV: it won’t work over mobile data or away from home.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to install anything on the TV?

No. Comandoo uses the network features your TV already has. The TV just needs to be on and on the same Wi-Fi as your phone.

Does it work over mobile data or away from home?

No. Your phone has to be on the same Wi-Fi as the TV, because commands go directly from one device to the other.

My TV doesn’t show up. What should I do?

Check that the TV is on and that both devices are on the same Wi-Fi (some routers split 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz into separate networks). If it still doesn’t appear, send a report from the app with your TV’s brand and model.

Is the app paid?

No. The remote is free and has no ads. In the future, some extra features may become part of an optional plan, but the basic remote stays free forever.

Does it work with any TV?

It works with Wi-Fi-connected Smart TVs on the platforms listed above. TVs without a network connection, or that only accept infrared remotes, won’t work.

Who makes Comandoo?

Agência D3, from Belém, Brazil. Comandoo is an independent app with no ties to any TV manufacturer.
